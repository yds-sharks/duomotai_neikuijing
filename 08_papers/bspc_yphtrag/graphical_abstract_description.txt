Overview of YpathRAG. A dual-channel hybrid retriever (dense + lexicon-guided sparse) retrieves a fixed top-k1 candidate pool, followed by a Supportive Evidence Verifier (SEV) that reranks within the pool using dual-head predictions (supportive vs. non-supportive and graded support strength), producing top-k2 supportive evidence for downstream pathology question answering without increasing retrieval depth.
